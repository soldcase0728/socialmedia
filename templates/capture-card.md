```
Ordinary classroom -- every teacher, one class period (8 min)
Why: baseline daily capture -- the library only grows if this happens
  [ ] 5 vertical clips, 5-10 seconds each, of students actually working
  [ ] 1 teacher-student interaction -- a question being answered at a desk
  [ ] 1 wide shot of the room
  [ ] 3 student reaction shots
  [ ] 1 student saying in one sentence what he is doing and why

```

**Rules:** vertical · get closer · do not ask anyone to look at the camera ·
ten seconds of one thing beats sixty seconds of everything · if a student is not
cleared for photos, do not film him · upload before you leave the room.
