# Weekly Intelligence Report

Generated by `python -m brandops weekly --learn`. Circulated Friday.

## Structure

**Winners / Losers** -- by index against our own median, not against an industry
benchmark.

**Observed evidence** -- numbers only, no explanation attached.

**Strategic inference** -- clearly labeled unproven, gated on sample size. Below
three observations the report says "too small to act on" rather than inventing a
lesson.

**Pillar performance** -- which brand messages are landing.

**Creative performance** -- which hooks, formats and subjects are working.

**Next week** -- what changes, specifically.

**Capture assignments** -- what to photograph or film, by whom.

## Reading it honestly

- One good post is not a pattern. Three are a hypothesis. Ten are a rule.
- A pillar underperforming usually means weak *evidence in the footage*, not a
  weak message. Check the shots before changing the strategy.
- If no admissions actions were attributed, fix the measurement before drawing
  any conclusion about what converts.
- Reactions and follower counts are reported and ignored. They do not pay
  tuition.

## The monthly question

Once a month, ask the question the weekly report cannot: **what can St. Mary's
credibly own that no competitor can copy?** Then check whether last month's
content actually staked that claim, or merely filled slots.
