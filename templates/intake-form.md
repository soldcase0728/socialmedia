# Content Inbox -- Microsoft Form field list

Build as a **group form** owned by the marketing Microsoft 365 group so uploads
land in the group's SharePoint site rather than one person's OneDrive.

| # | Question | Type | Required | Help text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Upload your photos or video | File upload (allow multiple) | yes | Vertical video please. Do not rename anything. |
| 2 | What was happening? | Short text | yes | e.g. "AP Bio lab", "All-School Mass", "JV hockey practice" |
| 3 | Department, team or club | Choice | yes | list your departments; include "Other" |
| 4 | Your name | Short text | yes | So we can ask you a follow-up question |
| 5 | Describe it in one sentence | Long text | yes | What is in the shot? |
| 6 | When was it taken? | Date | no | Leave blank for today |
| 7 | Who is in it? | Short text | no | Student or faculty names, if you know them |
| 8 | Anything we would not know by looking at it? | Long text | no | The story behind the shot |
| 9 | Is this fine to post today? | Yes/No | no | Default yes |
| 10 | Anything sensitive here? | Yes/No | no | Discipline, privacy, health, family situations |
| 11 | Are all students shown cleared for photo release? | Yes/No | no | If you are not sure, say no |

Questions 1-5 are the entire ask. Everything else is optional and everything
after upload is automated. **Nobody renames a file, resizes a photo, writes a
caption or organizes a folder.**

Share panel -> QR code -> print it -> classroom doors, locker rooms, the
faculty room, the athletic office.
