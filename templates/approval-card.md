A blocked card cannot be approved. A named human clears the blocker first, and
the clearance is recorded with their name. Approval always requires a named
person -- there is no system approval.

**Approver's four questions:**

1. Is every fact in this true and checkable?
2. Is every student in it cleared, and would his parents be glad to see it?
3. Does the footage prove the pillar, or does the caption merely assert it?
4. Would we be comfortable if this were the only thing a family saw of us?
