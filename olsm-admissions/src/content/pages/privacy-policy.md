## What this site collects

- **What you type into a form.** Parent and student names, grade, email or phone,
  how you heard about the school, and anything you write in the message field.
- **How you got here.** The campaign tags on the link you clicked (`utm_source`,
  `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) and the page you submitted from.
- **Anonymous usage measurement.** Google Analytics 4 and Vercel Analytics record
  page views and events. TODO: confirm — whether the Meta Pixel is enabled in
  production and what it is used for.

## What we do with it

Admissions staff read every inquiry and reply. Submissions are stored in a
Google Sheet and emailed to the admissions office. RSVP counts are used to plan
seating and parking.

We do not sell information collected on this site.
