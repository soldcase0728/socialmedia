## 1. Inquire

Send the [inquiry form](/#inquiry) or call the admissions office. You will hear back
from a person within one school hour. Tell us the student's current school and the
grade they are entering; that determines everything that follows.

## 2. Visit

Book a [shadow day](/shadow-days) or come to an [open house](/events-on-campus).
Shadow days put a student in real classes with a student host for a full day.
Open houses are for the whole family and run about two hours.
