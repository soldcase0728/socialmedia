## The five shots

**1 · ARRIVAL — 7:25–8:00 a.m.**
Ordinary morning. Students walking in. An adult holding a door and saying a name. Nobody looks at you.
*Who:* 7th/8th foreground, 11th/12th background. Log **[NAME], grade** for at least two.
*Where:* lakeside approach → brick path → main entrance → lockers.
*Format:* vertical 9:16 video + one 3:2 horizontal still.

**2 · [10:05] — THE ROOM — be seated and rolling BEFORE it fills**
Both divisions seated. Get a clock or the bell panel in one frame. Get one frame with a 7th grader and a senior in it, both facing forward — never facing each other.
*Who:* name the adult at the front. Near rows 7/8, back rows 11/12.
*Where:* [chapel/auditorium] — from the back, from the aisle at row height, one reverse from the front.
*Format:* vertical video, 3 angles + 1 horizontal still.

**3 · THE WORK — 1:10–1:45 p.m.**
Overhead, 30 inches up, window light from camera left. Marked paper with **one crossed-out line** and a teacher's handwritten *sentence* in the margin. Student's hands and pen in frame.
*Who:* hands only — no face needed, so no consent barrier. Still log [NAME], grade.
*Where:* [Room 214], the real desk, grain and all.
*Format:* 4:5 still + 3:2 still.
**No laptop. No tablet. No screen.**

**4 · THE QUESTION — 9:15–10:00 a.m.**
A real question written on a board, and a class working on it. **Keep the silence. Keep the disagreement.** Do not run it twice.
*Who:* 7th/8th + the named adult.
*Where:* [Room 214] with the window and brick outside.
*Format:* vertical video. Two cameras if possible — wide on sticks, handheld for faces.

**5 · 4:20 — 4:15–4:35 p.m.**
Campus late afternoon with people still in it. Practice. A ride that hasn't come. Someone still in a classroom.
*Who:* mixed grades, people small in frame but clearly people.
*Where:* lake behind, brick in frame.
*Format:* 4:5 still + 3:2 horizontal.

---

## Do not shoot

Posed lineups · drone orbits of an empty campus · anyone smiling into the lens on cue · a senior with a hand on a 7th grader's shoulder · crucifix close-ups · laptops standing in for academics · **anything you had to ask someone to do a second time.**

---

## If Summit / Formation is cancelled

1. **Shoot the empty room at [10:05] anyway.** Wide from the back, chairs, light, clock visible. It becomes a Story with an honest caption.
2. **Substitute a genuinely mixed-division period** — [choir / band / shared elective / service block / morning prayer] — and tell the editor what it actually is. **Never label another period "Formation."**
3. **Promote shot #3 to the day's hero.** The Work carries a day alone.
4. **The clock on screen must be the true clock.** If the type says [10:05], the shutter fired at [10:05].

---

## Consent — minors

- Shoot only students on the **current** media-release list. Get Thursday's refreshed version from [ADMIN NAME]. Not from memory.
- Anyone not on the list: **hands, backs and objects only.** Several of the strongest frames in this campaign have no face in them.
- **Two extra consents, beyond the standard release** — written, from parent *and* student, obtained **before** the shoot:
  - the **Day 17** student whose failing grade and retake are named;
  - any student **quoted by name** saying something contested in the Day 3 or Day 25 discussion.
- If a student asks you to stop, stop. Do not negotiate, and do not use what you already have of them.
- **No student's face in a Story carrying a location sticker. Ever.**
